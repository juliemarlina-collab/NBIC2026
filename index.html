About the Challenge

Where ideas become future-ready ventures.

NBIC 2026 is a prestigious national innovation and entrepreneurship competition organised by the Department of Polytechnic and Community College Education, Ministry of Higher Education, together with Politeknik Premier Port Dickson.

Participants gain national exposure, professional industry evaluation, strategic networking opportunities and a platform to showcase innovative products, services and business solutions with high commercial potential.

01

Showcase Innovation

Present a compelling idea, prototype, product or business solution to a national audience.

02

Industry Evaluation

Receive structured feedback and evaluation from experienced industry specialists and academics.

03

Build Connections

Expand your network with investors, educators, researchers and innovation ecosystem partners.
